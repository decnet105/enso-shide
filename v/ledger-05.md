# 80年代的中国，一辆自行车要花三到六个月工资

> 80年代的中国，一辆永久或凤凰自行车要花三到六个月工资，北京街头被称作自行车王国。这十年里还发生了几件事：1980年独生子女政策出台，1983年第一届春晚播出，1984年许海峰拿下中国第一枚奥运金牌，1987年第一家肯德基在北京开业。

Here are a few things about China in the 1980s that might surprise you. Back then, a bicycle was a luxury. A Forever or a Phoenix could cost three to six months of wages, and Beijing's streets were called a kingdom of bicycles. In 1980 came the one-child policy. Nineteen eighty-three brought the very first Spring Festival Gala, still must-watch every New Year's Eve. In 1984, Xu Haifeng won China's first Olympic gold. And in 1987, the first KFC opened in Beijing. A whole world, in just ten years.
