## Memory landmarks of the year

- **Beijing Olympic Games**（—）（Events · Aug）：Beijing 2008 opened Aug 8 at the Bird's Nest under 'One World, One Dream.' Zhang Yimou's ceremony and Li Ning's airborne torch-lighting dazzled; the host nation topped the medal table with 51 golds.
- **You and Me**（Liu Huan / Sarah Brightman）（Music · Aug）：The Beijing 2008 theme, sung at the opening by Liu Huan and Sarah Brightman atop a rising globe. Chen Qigang's gentle, almost whispered ballad—'You and Me, heart to heart'—chose intimacy over fanfare before billions of viewers.
- **Beijing Welcomes You**（100 singers）（Music · Apr）：Released for the 100-day Olympic countdown (Apr 2008), this all-star relay—Jackie Chan, Andy Lau, Na Ying, Wang Leehom and 100+ singers—greeted the world before Beijing's hutongs, the Great Wall and the Bird's Nest. Warm and ubiquitous, it became the summer's anthem.
- **Capricorn / Daoxiang**（Jay Chou）（Music · Oct）：Jay Chou's ninth album Capricorn (2008) and its lead single Dao Xiang, written amid the global financial crisis, became a healing anthem and later swept three Golden Melody Awards.
- **If You Are the One**（Feng Xiaogang / Ge You / Shu Qi）（Film · Dec）：Feng Xiaogang's 2008 New Year comedy: Ge You's overnight-rich bachelor 'interviews' brides, then finds love in Hokkaido (Shu Qi). Its snowy Dōtō scenery and quotable lines made it the year's box-office champ at ~325M yuan.
- **Kung Fu Panda**（DreamWorks）（Film · Jun）：DreamWorks' Kung Fu Panda (2008) made noodle-shop panda Po the unlikely Dragon Warrior; it earned 186M RMB in mainland China, the first animated film there to cross 100M.
- **Shenzhou-7: China's first spacewalk**（Zhai Zhigang）（Events · Sep）：Shenzhou-7 launched Sep 25, 2008. On Sep 27, Zhai Zhigang stepped out in China's domestic 'Feitian' suit and waved a national flag—China's first spacewalk, making it the third nation to master EVA independently. Crew landed Sep 28.
- **Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf**（Creative Power）（Anime）：Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf (first aired 2005) became a nationwide hit by 2008—wolf Wolffy's catchphrase about always coming back was a childhood staple—and grew into one of China's biggest homegrown animation IPs.
