Chinese Cultural Memory Timeline, 2008: what we watched, played and sang

Where were you that year? This timeline hand-picks 8 touchstones of Chinese collective memory from 2008 — TV, film, music, games and events — drawn from the Shide cultural database (2,400+ entries in Simplified/Traditional Chinese, English and Japanese, human-curated). Every entry is checkable, and any of them might be a doorway into your own story.

Memory landmarks of the year

  1. Beijing Olympic Games — —
    Events · Aug
    Beijing 2008 opened Aug 8 at the Bird's Nest under 'One World, One Dream.' Zhang Yimou's ceremony and Li Ning's airborne torch-lighting dazzled; the host nation topped the medal table with 51 golds.
  2. You and Me — Liu Huan / Sarah Brightman
    Music · Aug
    The Beijing 2008 theme, sung at the opening by Liu Huan and Sarah Brightman atop a rising globe. Chen Qigang's gentle, almost whispered ballad—'You and Me, heart to heart'—chose intimacy over fanfare before billions of viewers.
  3. Beijing Welcomes You — 100 singers
    Music · Apr
    Released for the 100-day Olympic countdown (Apr 2008), this all-star relay—Jackie Chan, Andy Lau, Na Ying, Wang Leehom and 100+ singers—greeted the world before Beijing's hutongs, the Great Wall and the Bird's Nest. Warm and ubiquitous, it became the summer's anthem.
  4. Capricorn / Daoxiang — Jay Chou
    Music · Oct
    Jay Chou's ninth album Capricorn (2008) and its lead single Dao Xiang, written amid the global financial crisis, became a healing anthem and later swept three Golden Melody Awards.
  5. If You Are the One — Feng Xiaogang / Ge You / Shu Qi
    Film · Dec
    Feng Xiaogang's 2008 New Year comedy: Ge You's overnight-rich bachelor 'interviews' brides, then finds love in Hokkaido (Shu Qi). Its snowy Dōtō scenery and quotable lines made it the year's box-office champ at ~325M yuan.
  6. Kung Fu Panda — DreamWorks
    Film · Jun
    DreamWorks' Kung Fu Panda (2008) made noodle-shop panda Po the unlikely Dragon Warrior; it earned 186M RMB in mainland China, the first animated film there to cross 100M.
  7. Shenzhou-7: China's first spacewalk — Zhai Zhigang
    Events · Sep
    Shenzhou-7 launched Sep 25, 2008. On Sep 27, Zhai Zhigang stepped out in China's domestic 'Feitian' suit and waved a national flag—China's first spacewalk, making it the third nation to master EVA independently. Crew landed Sep 28.
  8. Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf — Creative Power
    Anime
    Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf (first aired 2005) became a nationwide hit by 2008—wolf Wolffy's catchphrase about always coming back was a childhood staple—and grew into one of China's biggest homegrown animation IPs.
