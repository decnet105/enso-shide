## Memory landmarks of the year

- **My Fair Princess**（Qiong Yao / Vicki Zhao / Ruby Lin）（TV · Apr）：The 1998 Qiong Yao costume drama My Fair Princess made Vicki Zhao's mischievous Xiao Yanzi a sensation, peaking around 62.8% ratings; an endlessly-rerun summer staple across the Chinese-speaking world.
- **Meet in 1998**（Faye Wong / Na Ying）（Music · Jan）：At the 1998 CCTV Spring Festival Gala, Na Ying and Faye Wong's duet 'Meeting in 1998'—staged minimally in blue light—became one of the Gala's most replayed performances and a sonic landmark of the late-1990s.
- **Titanic (mainland release)**（James Cameron）（Film · Apr）：Titanic opened in mainland China on 3 Apr 1998 at premium 25–30 yuan tickets, grossing about 360M yuan—roughly a quarter of the year's national box office—and gave countless viewers their first cinema tears with 'You jump, I jump.'
- **Sing and Play / Red Bean**（Faye Wong）（Music · Oct）：Faye Wong's 1998 Mandarin album 'Sing & Play' birthed 'Red Bean,' an enduring national love ballad whose ethereal vocals made it a perennial KTV staple.
- **The Water Margin (CCTV)**（Zhang Shaolin / CCTV）（TV · Jan）：CCTV's 1998 'Water Margin' (43 episodes) capped its Four Classics adaptations; Liu Huan's theme 'Hero Song' became a national singalong and Li Xuejian's Song Jiang remains definitive.
- **StarCraft**（Blizzard）（Games · Mar）：Blizzard's StarCraft (Mar 1998), with its finely balanced Terran/Protoss/Zerg, redefined the RTS and—played all night in smoky Chinese internet cafés—served as the earliest spark for competitive gaming in China.
- **Be There or Be Square**（Feng Xiaogang / Ge You / Xu Fan）（Film · Dec）：Feng Xiaogang's 1998 New Year comedy set in Los Angeles, with Ge You and Xu Fan as overseas Chinese whose missed-then-found romance cemented the mainland 'New Year film' genre.
- **1998 FIFA World Cup (France)**（—）（Events · Jun）：France beat Brazil 3-0 to lift its first World Cup on 12 July 1998; Zidane, a midfield artist, sealed it with two headers (27' and 45+1')—still the only player to head twice in a final.
