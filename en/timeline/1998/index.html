Chinese Cultural Memory Timeline, 1998: what we watched, played and sang

Where were you that year? This timeline hand-picks 8 touchstones of Chinese collective memory from 1998 — TV, film, music, games and events — drawn from the Shide cultural database (2,400+ entries in Simplified/Traditional Chinese, English and Japanese, human-curated). Every entry is checkable, and any of them might be a doorway into your own story.

Memory landmarks of the year

  1. My Fair Princess — Qiong Yao / Vicki Zhao / Ruby Lin
    TV · Apr
    The 1998 Qiong Yao costume drama My Fair Princess made Vicki Zhao's mischievous Xiao Yanzi a sensation, peaking around 62.8% ratings; an endlessly-rerun summer staple across the Chinese-speaking world.
  2. Meet in 1998 — Faye Wong / Na Ying
    Music · Jan
    At the 1998 CCTV Spring Festival Gala, Na Ying and Faye Wong's duet 'Meeting in 1998'—staged minimally in blue light—became one of the Gala's most replayed performances and a sonic landmark of the late-1990s.
  3. Titanic (mainland release) — James Cameron
    Film · Apr
    Titanic opened in mainland China on 3 Apr 1998 at premium 25–30 yuan tickets, grossing about 360M yuan—roughly a quarter of the year's national box office—and gave countless viewers their first cinema tears with 'You jump, I jump.'
  4. Sing and Play / Red Bean — Faye Wong
    Music · Oct
    Faye Wong's 1998 Mandarin album 'Sing & Play' birthed 'Red Bean,' an enduring national love ballad whose ethereal vocals made it a perennial KTV staple.
  5. The Water Margin (CCTV) — Zhang Shaolin / CCTV
    TV · Jan
    CCTV's 1998 'Water Margin' (43 episodes) capped its Four Classics adaptations; Liu Huan's theme 'Hero Song' became a national singalong and Li Xuejian's Song Jiang remains definitive.
  6. StarCraft — Blizzard
    Games · Mar
    Blizzard's StarCraft (Mar 1998), with its finely balanced Terran/Protoss/Zerg, redefined the RTS and—played all night in smoky Chinese internet cafés—served as the earliest spark for competitive gaming in China.
  7. Be There or Be Square — Feng Xiaogang / Ge You / Xu Fan
    Film · Dec
    Feng Xiaogang's 1998 New Year comedy set in Los Angeles, with Ge You and Xu Fan as overseas Chinese whose missed-then-found romance cemented the mainland 'New Year film' genre.
  8. 1998 FIFA World Cup (France) — —
    Events · Jun
    France beat Brazil 3-0 to lift its first World Cup on 12 July 1998; Zidane, a midfield artist, sealed it with two headers (27' and 45+1')—still the only player to head twice in a final.
