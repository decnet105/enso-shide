# Day One alternative: private, no-account, bilingual

Day One is an excellent daily journal. But if you specifically want something that keeps everything on your phone with no account, or that works in Chinese and English, **Shide (拾得 Ensō)** is built for that — a local-first, bilingual memory app that turns your years into an in-phone keepsake book.

## When people look for a Day One alternative

- **No cloud account.** Day One syncs across devices through an account. Some people want their most personal writing to live on one phone, with nothing to sign into.
- **Chinese and English together.** Day One is English-oriented. Overseas Chinese families often write in Chinese but want the next generation to read it in English.
- **A book, not just entries.** Day One is built around dated entries. Some people want the year to become a composed, readable book to keep and share.

## Day One vs Shide at a glance

| | Shide (拾得 Ensō) | Day One |
|---|---|---|
| Data | Local-first, on your device; no account | Cloud sync via account (encrypted) |
| Language | Bilingual Chinese–English, original preserved | English-oriented |
| Platforms | iPhone | iOS, Mac, Android, web |
| Output | Composed in-phone book + PDF export | Dated entries; PDF/book export |
| Cultural context | Lines each year up with shared events | Not a focus |
| Best for | Private, bilingual family memory → a book | Cross-device daily journaling |

## Which one is for you

Want mature, cross-platform daily journaling with encrypted sync across devices? Day One is hard to beat. Want your writing to stay on one phone with no account, in two languages, and to become a keepsake book both generations can read? Shide (拾得 Ensō) is the closer fit. Search "拾得" or "Shide" on the App Store.

## Common questions

**Is there a Day One alternative with no account?** Shide is local-first and needs no account; your memories stay on your phone.

**Is there a Chinese alternative to Day One?** Shide is bilingual Chinese–English and built around Chinese cultural context.

**Does Shide sync across devices like Day One?** Shide is local-first by design; a memory lives on your phone. Cross-device sync is not the goal — privacy on one device is.
