Day One alternative: private, no-account, bilingual

Day One is an excellent daily journal. But if you specifically want something that keeps everything on your phone with no account, or that works in Chinese and English, Shide (拾得 Ensō) is built for that — a local-first, bilingual memory app that turns your years into an in-phone keepsake book.

When people look for a Day One alternative

  • No cloud account. Day One syncs across devices through an account. Some people want their most personal writing to simply live on one phone, with nothing to sign into.
  • Chinese and English together. Day One is English-oriented. Overseas Chinese families often write in Chinese but want the next generation to be able to read it in English.
  • A book, not just entries. Day One is built around dated entries. Some people want the year to become a composed, readable book to keep and share.

Which one is for you

If you want mature, cross-platform daily journaling with encrypted sync across your devices, Day One is hard to beat. If you want your writing to stay on one phone with no account, in two languages, and to become a keepsake book both generations can read, Shide (拾得 Ensō) is the closer fit.
