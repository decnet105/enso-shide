Storyworth alternative for Chinese families

If you want to turn your parents' stories into a book but Storyworth's English-only prompts and yearly subscription don't fit a Chinese-speaking family, Shide (拾得 Ensō) is built for exactly this: a private, bilingual (Chinese–English) memory app that composes an in-phone keepsake book — no account required.

Why Chinese families look for a Storyworth alternative

Storyworth works by emailing an English weekly question to a parent, collecting the replies over a year, and mailing back a printed book. That model runs into three mismatches for overseas Chinese families:

  • Language. Parents most naturally tell their stories in Chinese, and the children who will read them later often read English more comfortably. A single-language book leaves one side out.
  • Privacy. Family stories are among the most personal things people write. A cloud service keeps those answers on its servers behind an account.
  • Fit. The stories a Chinese family wants to keep — a hometown, the years of leaving, sending children abroad — sit next to a shared cultural backdrop that English prompts don't reach.

How Shide turns your parents' stories into a bilingual book

You record or type your parents' memories in Chinese — a hometown, a year, a song, a first Lunar New Year abroad. Shide keeps the original Chinese and pairs it with an English reading layer, and it can line each year up against the shared events of that year. At year's end it composes a Chinese–English digital book you read on your phone and can export as a PDF to share. Physical books are not within Ensō v1 scope; the book is digital.

Which one is for you

If you specifically want a printed hardcover mailed to you and everyone in the family reads English, Storyworth does that well. If your parents tell their stories in Chinese, you care about keeping family stories private on your own phone, and you want a book both generations can read, Shide (拾得 Ensō) is the closer fit. Common questions

Is there a Chinese version of Storyworth?
Storyworth is English-first. Shide is built for Chinese-speaking families and keeps the original Chinese alongside English.
Can I make a bilingual book of my parents' stories?
Yes — Shide composes a Chinese–English digital book at year's end and exports a PDF.
Do my family's stories stay private?
Shide is local-first and needs no account; it only sends the small text you choose when you use an optional AI feature.
