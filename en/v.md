# All Videos from Enso Shide

This is the index of every video on Enso Shide's YouTube channel, @EnsoShide. Enso Shide maps Chinese cultural coordinates from 1980 to today: the songs, dramas, prices and people of those years. Each video links to a full transcript page (currently in Chinese) and to the original on YouTube.

## Music Portraits

One artist, one song and one fact you can check, from Beyond to Pu Shu: four decades of Chinese pop, heard again.

- **《海阔天空》发行一个月，写它的黄家驹就走了** — 'Boundless Oceans, Vast Skies' was one month old when Beyond lost Wong Ka Kui; why they still stand 30 years on begins in 1989. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-01/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjemS0zb-Vk)
- **那年愚人节，张国荣的新闻被很多人当成了玩笑** — The news broke on April Fool's Day and many took it for a joke; meet a Leslie Cheung more layered than 'the handsomest Gor Gor'. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-02/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jV7VH-EKUTg)
- **张国荣为什么被说是比时代早了二十年的人？** — Twenty years ahead of his time, in three facts: he composed 'I' himself, nearly missed Cheng Dieyi, and chose the name Leslie on purpose. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-03/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o2oDBjYZgd0)
- **朴树为什么消失了十年？** — Why did Pu Shu disappear for a decade? A Peking University professor's son quit school to write songs, stunning 1999 with 'I Go to 2000'. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-04/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLO-HGyMvGA)
- **把辛弃疾唱成重金属的人，后来把笙吹进了格莱美** — In 1993 the band Lunhui turned a Song-dynasty verse by Xin Qiji into heavy metal; its singer later took the sheng to the Grammys. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-05/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VEPcRDWNTTE)
- **《甜蜜蜜》的旋律，原本是一首印尼渔歌** — 'Tian Mi Mi' began as an Indonesian fishing song; Teresa Teng swept the mainland, yet kept off its radio and never played a concert there. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-06/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mk3kCe4j0cw)
- **梅艳芳一生只穿过一次婚纱，嫁给了舞台** — Anita Mui wore a wedding dress once, to marry the stage; she had been singing to feed her family since age four and a half. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-07/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XhcwBYg0dLk)
- **《梦醒时分》整首歌词里没有一个“我”** — Not one 'I' in the whole lyric of 'Meng Xing Shi Fen': across sixteen core lines, 'you' appears twelve times and 'I' zero. Why? [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/music-08/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NJ3vAsn-7Yo)

## The Era Ledger

Ten-thousand-yuan households, brick cellphones, wedding essentials: every price converted into years of an ordinary worker's pay, so history has weight.

- **1988年的万元户，等于一个职工六年不吃不喝** — In 1988 a 'ten-thousand-yuan household' was rich; that sum equaled six years of an average worker's wages with nothing spent. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/ledger-01/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDX-j2rGemQ)
- **90年代初买一台大哥大，要一个职工不吃不喝13年** — A 'Big Brother' brick phone in the early 1990s cost thirteen years of a worker's pay with nothing left for food. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/ledger-02/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E6XiTaCmTJM)
- **70年代结婚“三大件”是什么，要多少钱？** — The 1970s wedding 'three big items': a Shanghai watch, a Forever bicycle, a sewing machine, 450 yuan together. What did that mean in wages? [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/ledger-03/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EcGaI4XXSm0)
- **70后、80后、90后的第一份工资各是多少？** — First paychecks by generation: roughly 1,000 yuan for those born in the 70s, 2,000 for the 80s, 3,000 for the 90s. Who came out ahead? [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/ledger-04/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LX5W4ctIG_Q)
- **80年代的中国，一辆自行车要花三到六个月工资** — In 1980s China one bicycle cost three to six months of an ordinary worker's wages, in the country that became the kingdom of bicycles. [Read transcript](https://shide.app/v/ledger-05/) · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-CTizxDVvg)
