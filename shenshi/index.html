关于 · About

沈拾，二十七岁，钟表修复师。她修停摆的钟，也修被删改先后的历史——那些被压成「若干」和「无名」的普通人，她替他们把走过的时刻放回原位，唱给你听。

她是「拾得 Ensō」世界观里的虚构角色，由 AI 创作——一个不存在的人，替存在过的人作证。尘埃里抬头，把光传下去。

Shen Shi is not a real person — and that's the point. She is a fictional character from the Enso Shide story universe, a 27-year-old restorer of stopped clocks and rewritten timelines, performed entirely with AI. She sings for the ordinary people history erased into "nobody" — the ones filed under "unknown," whose before and after were quietly reordered. Testimony, not tearjerkers. Look up from the dust, and pass the light on.
